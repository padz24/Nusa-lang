Formatter (nusa fmt)

v3.0.0

Formatter token-based idempoten: nusa fmt file.ns, cek via --check. Aturan: bar |x| menempel, ? menempel, arm match diformat, komentar doc /// DIPERTAHANKAN (regresi 2.8.0).

./build/nusa fmt --check examples/hello.ns
./build/nusa fmt tutorials/01-hello/main.ns
